Privacy Policy

リサーチ・ダイジェスト (the “Site”) is operated by Riorina (the “Operator”) and introduces the headlines and summaries of publicly available research and reports. This policy sets out how the Site handles information. Last updated: 2026-11-07

1. Information we collect

  • Account (only if you use My page): You log in with an email address and password, or with a Google account. What we obtain is your email address (for Google, the email address Google provides). Passwords are stored encrypted (hashed) and cannot be seen by the Operator. You do not need to log in just to read articles, events and the glossary.
  • My page content (marks, word books, memos) is saved to the authentication and database service “Supabase”, linked to the account you logged in with. It can be read and written only by your own account and is not visible to other users. To keep you logged in, authentication information is stored in your browser’s localStorage. When you log out, the My page content on that device is erased. The Operator does not view the content, except for service maintenance or where required by law.
  • Deleting your account: You can delete your account and saved content at any time from “Delete account” on My page.
  • Access information: The Site’s delivery provider (GitHub Pages) may record access logs (IP address, browser information, etc.). The Operator does not use them to identify individuals.

2. Cookies and advertising

  • The Site may use the advertising service “Google AdSense” (only while ads are displayed).
  • Third-party ad providers such as Google use cookies to serve ads based on a user’s past visits to this Site and other sites.
  • By using advertising cookies, Google and its partners can show users appropriate ads based on their visits to this Site and other sites.
  • If you choose “Decline” in the notice shown on your first visit, ads are not loaded (and no advertising cookies are set). You can change your choice at any time from “Cookie settings” at the bottom of the page.
  • You can turn off personalised ads in Google’s Ads Settings. You can also opt out of third-party ad providers’ cookies at aboutads.info.
  • For how Google handles data, see Google’s Privacy & Terms.

3. Analytics and view counts

To show popular reports, the Site records how many times a report’s original link is opened, as totals by type, ID and date (stored in the Operator’s tally database on Supabase). A single browser is counted at most once per day for the same report. No personally identifying information (IP address, name, email address, etc.) is stored in these counts.

To understand traffic, the Site may use cookie-free, non-identifying analytics (Cloudflare Web Analytics). Even then, only statistical information such as the number of pages viewed is obtained.

4. Provision to third parties and outsourcing

Except where required by law, we do not provide the information we obtain to third parties. However, for login and data storage we use Supabase Inc. (data may be stored on servers outside Japan) and, if you log in with Google, the authentication service of Google LLC. The privacy policies of these providers also apply.

External transmission (disclosure under Japan’s Telecommunications Business Act)

On this Site, information may be sent from your device to the following external services.

  • Google AdSense (Google LLC): for serving ads. Identifiers such as cookies, pages viewed, and device/browser information. Only when ads are displayed and you choose “Agree”.
  • Cloudflare Web Analytics (Cloudflare, Inc.): to understand traffic. Statistical information such as pages viewed, referrer and browser type (no cookies). Only when used.
  • Supabase (Supabase Inc.): for login and saving My page. Your email address and My page content. Only when you are logged in.
  • Google login authentication (Google LLC): only when you choose “Log in with Google”.
  • Cloudflare (Cloudflare, Inc.): for delivering (hosting) the Site. Your IP address and similar data are processed when you access it.

5. Inquiries and removal requests

For questions about this policy or the content on the Site, please use the Operator & Contact page.

6. Changes

We may revise this policy as needed. A revision takes effect when it is posted on this page.
