A Word from the Creator

Thank you for stopping by this site.

Before you start

This is the first website I have ever built, so there may be rough edges or things that are inconvenient to use. I would be grateful if you could bear with me.

If anything bothers you, or if you have questions or feedback about the site, please send them my way. They are a valuable starting point for making the site better. You can reach me through the Operator & Contact page (in Japanese).

Why I made this site

Economic reports are published by many different institutions, yet there is hardly any site or system that gathers them in one place and lets you look only at the ones that catch your interest. I wanted to make that easy, so that even people outside the world of economics can find a way in to the latest economic trends and developments. That is why I built this site.

To be honest, I also wanted to try building a website with AI, and I wanted a quick way to browse economic reports and articles by genre. Both were driving forces behind this project.

Going forward

I would be happy if many people visited, but I know the site is not at that level yet. For now, I am building it first of all for my own use. If there are people who find it useful in the same way, I hope to grow it into a site that satisfies them too.

Note: the site's content (summaries and explanations) is currently in Japanese.
